Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

SENETRACK is an independent civic-technology platform. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a visitor.

1 · Who We Are

SENETRACK is an independent, non-partisan civic-technology platform that tracks and analyses the parliamentary performance of Kenya Senate senators using publicly available official records. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or funded by the Parliament of Kenya, the Senate, or any political party.

2 · Data We Collect

We collect the minimum data necessary to operate the platform:

  • Contact form submissions — name, optional email, subject, and message body, submitted voluntarily via the feedback form on the About page. These are stored securely and used only to respond to your query.
  • IP addresses — recorded at the time of a contact form submission for spam and abuse prevention. IP addresses are not linked to browsing activity.
  • Anonymous analytics — if enabled, we use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-first, cookie-free analytics tool. Plausible does not use cookies, does not track individuals across sites, and does not collect personal data. Aggregate page-view statistics only.

3 · Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not use advertising trackers or third-party marketing cookies.
  • We do not build user profiles or track individuals across sessions.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with any third party for commercial purposes.
  • We do not require account creation or login to use any part of the platform.

4 · Senator & Parliamentary Data

All senator performance data displayed on SENETRACK is sourced from official public records — the Kenya Hansard, Parliament of Kenya attendance registers, division lists (via Mzalendo), and the official Statements Tracker. This data is in the public domain and its publication here does not constitute a privacy violation.

Senator profile photographs displayed on this platform are sourced from publicly available parliamentary records or public domain sources. If you are a senator or representative and believe an image has been used incorrectly, please contact us via the feedback form.

5 · Cookies

SENETRACK does not set any tracking or advertising cookies. The only browser storage used is for essential site functionality (e.g. the PWA service worker for offline caching of static assets). No personal information is stored in browser storage.

6 · Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained for a reasonable period to allow us to respond to queries and track data corrections. IP addresses logged for spam prevention are retained for a maximum of 90 days and then deleted. We do not retain any other personal data.

7 · Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of personal data you have submitted (e.g. a contact form message).
  • Object to any processing of your personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the feedback form on the About page.

8 · Third-Party Services

  • Cloudinary — senator and party images are hosted on Cloudinary's CDN for fast delivery. Images served via Cloudinary are subject to Cloudinary's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts CDN. Google may log font requests; see Google's privacy policy.
  • Plausible Analytics (if enabled) — privacy-first, no personal data collected. See Plausible's privacy policy.

9 · Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of SENETRACK after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10 · Contact

For any privacy-related queries, data requests, or concerns, please use the feedback form on the About page. We aim to respond within 14 days.
