3 · Data & Accuracy

All senator performance data is sourced from official public records — the Kenya Hansard, Parliament of Kenya attendance registers, division lists (via Mzalendo), and the official Statements Tracker. While we make every effort to ensure accuracy, SENETRACK cannot guarantee that all data is complete, current, or free of errors.

SPM scores are algorithmic proxies computed from measurable parliamentary activity. They are indicative only and do not constitute a comprehensive or definitive assessment of a senator's effectiveness, character, or contribution to public life. Scores are subject to revision as better data becomes available.

If you identify a data error, please report it via the feedback form on the About page.
